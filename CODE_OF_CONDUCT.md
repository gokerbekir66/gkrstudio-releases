# Code of conduct

GKR Studio's issues and discussions are a place to report problems, ask questions and suggest ideas. Everyone is welcome, whatever their experience, language, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here write in their second or third language.
- Stay on topic and assume good intentions.
- Give the details others need to help you (version, operating system, steps).
- Accept that not every request can be built.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats or discriminatory language.
- Sexual content or unwanted attention.
- Sharing someone else's private information.
- Spam, advertising or links to malware or pirated content.
- Asking for help to break the law or other people's rights (for example downloading content you are not allowed to download).

## What happens

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close or lock issues, and block accounts that break these rules. Serious or repeated cases lead to a permanent block.

## Reporting

Report a comment with GitHub's **⋯ → Report content** menu, or open an issue titled "Conduct" with a link (leave out private details). Security problems go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
